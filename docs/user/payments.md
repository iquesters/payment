# Taking payments

## Set up (once per organisation)

**Payment settings** (org-admins and super-admins) → Edit:

- **Take payments by UPI**: the UPI ID and the payee name exactly as the UPI app shows them. A committee member's UPI ID is fine until the organisation's bank account is ready. Optional instructions appear next to the QR.
- **Take cash**: admins record cash themselves (below).
- **Refund and cancellation policy**: shown on every pay page.

Pick another organisation from the list at the top if you administer more than one.

## How someone pays

1. After registering (or buying a membership) they reach the **pay page** of their order, e.g. reference `TTV-MEET-0042`.
2. They scan the **QR** with any UPI app (on a phone: **Open in a UPI app**). The amount and the reference are filled in.
3. They enter the **transaction ID (UTR)**, the 12-digit number in the UPI app's payment details, and attach a **screenshot**.
4. The order shows **Payment under review** until an admin checks it.

## Verify payments

**Payments** lists what people sent, waiting ones first. Open one and compare with the bank statement or the UPI app's history:

- the **amount** (shown in red if it differs from the order total),
- the **UTR**, and the **reference** in the payment note.

Then choose **Verify** (the order is paid, the registration or membership goes ahead) or **Reject** with a reason. The payer sees the reason on their pay page and can send the details again.

## Cash or UPI at the desk

**Payments → Record a payment**: the order reference, Cash or UPI, the amount received (and the UTR for UPI). It is verified at once and the order is paid. If the amount is less than the total, add a note why.
